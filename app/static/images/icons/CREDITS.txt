Icons under subjects/, items/, and the badges in ranks/ and
achievements/, are extracted from "Raven Fantasy Icons" by Clockwork
Raven Studios (Caio), purchased on itch.io. No explicit license text
shipped with the pack beyond a thank-you note from the artist —
itch.io "buy once, use in your projects" packs of this kind typically
allow embedding in a shipped game/app but forbid reselling the raw
assets standalone. Double-check the itch.io purchase terms before
shipping to production.
