Icons under ui/ are extracted from the "UI Bundle Free" pack (itch.io,
free asset bundle covering several UI themes — casino, horror, pastel,
cozy-café, bamboo/nature, and this one: a medieval fantasy set). Only
the medieval-fantasy-themed sheet was used, since it's the only one
that actually matches this app's own visual language; the others
(casino, horror, etc.) were left untouched — not appropriate for an
educational app aimed at kids. No explicit license file shipped beyond
the bundle itself; treat under the same "buy once/free download, use
in your own project" assumption as the Raven Fantasy Icons pack (see
../CREDITS.txt) — double-check before shipping to production.

  pergaminho-suspenso.png   hanging wooden scroll/sign, used as a small
                             decorative flourish above the login/register
                             panel (see app/templates/auth/login.html and
                             register.html) — cropped from the pack's
                             "freefantasy.png" full spritesheet (source
                             region: x 87-156, y 41-163, out of a 256x256
                             sheet). The raw spritesheet is kept locally
                             at app/static/assets/uibundlefree/ (not
                             committed — see .gitignore) in case a future
                             pass wants to pull more elements from it
                             (the sheet also has PLAY/LOAD/EXIT scroll
                             buttons, small icon buttons, gem badges, and
                             colored candles not currently used anywhere).

  nav-play.png              small carved wooden "play" triangle button
                             (source region: x 65-79, y 177-191 of the
                             same freefantasy.png sheet) — used as the
                             prev/next icon for the two chapter/chronicle
                             carousels (mathematics/chronicles.html's map
                             carousel and chronicle_detail.html's chapter
                             reader), replacing the generic FontAwesome
                             chevron. "prev" reuses the same image
                             mirrored via CSS (transform: scaleX(-1))
                             rather than a separate asset — it's a
                             symmetric-enough triangle that mirroring
                             reads cleanly as "point the other way".

  check-icon.png,           small carved wooden check/X badges (same
  x-icon.png                sheet, rows just below nav-play.png) — used
                             in the concept-exercise answer feedback
                             banner (mathematics/_concept_question.html)
                             instead of a small inline FontAwesome glyph.
                             Deliberately NOT used in the main battle
                             loop's correct/wrong bubbles (battle-arena.js)
                             — that fires on every single answer in the
                             game's highest-traffic screen, where a
                             misaligned inline image would be far more
                             noticeable/annoying than in the concept
                             exercise's dedicated, lower-traffic banner.
